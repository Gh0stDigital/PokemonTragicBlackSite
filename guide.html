OFFICIAL FIELD GUIDE

WALKTHROUGHA GUIDE FOR LOST TRAINERS

Chapter notes, tips, and gentle hints. This guide stays spoiler-light by default — endings and major puzzle solutions are hidden behind clearly-marked reveals.

/// BEFORE YOU START

Installing the patch SETUP

Supply your own legally obtained base ROM, then apply the release patch with a compatible patcher. Full steps and the verified ROM hash will appear on the Download page at launch.

A Familiar Journey EXPLORATION

Your journey begins much like the original Pokémon Red, but the familiar path is not necessarily the only one.

Most of Kanto can be explored in its traditional order, with adjustments that allow greater freedom early in the game. Don't assume every obstacle requires the same solution you remember.

Training Is Optional GAMEPLAY

Unlike traditional Pokémon games, catching, training, and grinding Pokémon are not strictly required to complete your journey.

A conventional team-based approach remains available, but alternative methods of progression exist.

How you choose to play is up to you.

The Power of GHOST MECHANICS

GHOST offers possibilities beyond those of an ordinary Pokémon.

Its use can influence the world, the people you encounter, and the direction your story takes.

Consider your actions carefully. Not every consequence will be immediately apparent.

Death Has Consequences WARNING

Pokémon Tragic Black features permadeath mechanics. Certain actions or failures can permanently end your journey and erase your save data.

There may be no opportunity to undo what has happened.

See General Tips if you are unsure about specific outcomes. Warning: those tips are not totally spoiler free.

Save States RECOMMENDED

For players seeking a more forgiving experience, emulator save states are strongly recommended.

Save states can serve as an unofficial easy mode, allowing you to return to earlier moments without restarting your entire journey.

For the intended high-stakes experience, play without relying on them.

/// CHAPTER NOTES

Spoiler policy: General guidance is shown openly. Anything that could spoil a twist, ending, or puzzle solution is tucked inside a “Reveal” dropdown — open it only when you're stuck.

CHAPTER 01 — Start of Journey

Early route choice that determines whether you receive GHOST.

REVEAL — Chapter 01 details

At the start of your journey, when you initially leave your home, you will be approached by a gentleman NPC who will present you with a question. Answer with the first response if you wish to acquire GHOST, this ROM hack's core mechanic.

Choosing the other option will result in not receiving GHOST, and the game will play more like the vanilla game (however, this doesn't mean it's the last time you may see him). It is recommended that you take the GHOST path for the strongest intended experience.

CHAPTER 02 — Mt. Moon Activity

Critical fossil decision point tied to endings.

REVEAL — Chapter 02 details

At Mt. Moon, when you battle the scientist guarding the Pokémon fossils, it is highly recommended that you use GHOST to ensure you can claim both the Dome and Helix Fossils. Failing to do so will lock the player out of one of the game's most interesting endings.

CHAPTER 03 — Cerulean Shortcut

Find a familiar NPC in Cerulean for a major progression gift.

REVEAL — Chapter 03 details

When you arrive in Cerulean, you should visit all the homes to find a familiar NPC who, when talked to, will award the player with something very useful for getting through the game more quickly. It is also a requirement for one of the endings.

CHAPTER 04 — Pokémon Tower Terrorist

Tower route caution: team setup and Silph Scope alternatives.

REVEAL — Chapter 04 details

When going through Pokémon Tower, it is recommended that you have other Pokémon besides GHOST. If you received the gift from the NPC in Cerulean, then you won't have to worry about this, and you can even complete Pokémon Tower without the Silph Scope.

CHAPTER 05 — An Unfortunate Fate

Prompt to revisit Pokémon Tower after the Fuji event.

REVEAL — Chapter 05 details

After clearing Pokémon Tower and saving Fuji, he will mention that you may want to revisit the tower to meet someone else, so it may be worth your time to do so right away.

CHAPTER 06 — The Perfect Container

Master Ball and progression-order warning for an ending route.

REVEAL — Chapter 06 details

The Master Ball is precious and is also used for a key questline for one of the endings, so be sure to hold on to it. You will know when.

Silph Co. can be completed early if you received the gift from Cerulean and cleared Pokémon Tower first. You can skip the Game Corner Rocket Hideout entirely this way.

CHAPTER 07 — Insatiable Appetite

GHOST level cap matters for multiple endings.

REVEAL — Chapter 07 details

GHOST's max level is 50, and reaching it is a requirement for several endings. This can be done relatively quickly, depending on how much you use GHOST.

CHAPTER 08 — A Fated Encounter

Cinnabar Mansion event tied to the Cerulean gift path.

REVEAL — Chapter 08 details

Be sure to visit the Pokémon Mansion on Cinnabar Island to meet an old friend, but only if you received the gift in Cerulean.

CHAPTER 09 — Choose Your Wife!?

Questline branch based on Gym Leader relationship outcomes.

REVEAL — Chapter 09 details

There is a part of a questline that requires you to choose one of the female Gym Leaders as a partner. They must have been left alive and must not dislike you for your actions (maybe don't hurt the Gym members). Later, you can choose one to be your partner.

CHAPTER 10 — The Black Tamer

Black Tamer route unlocks expanded ability usage.

REVEAL — Chapter 10 details

If you have completed the ritual and become the Black Tamer, you will have access to a series of abilities that can be used both in and outside of battle. See General Tips for further explanation of them.

CHAPTER 11 — Remove the Outsiders

League encounters are significantly tougher on Black Tamer path.

REVEAL — Chapter 11 details

When facing the Pokémon League, you may notice very quickly that it's not as simple as many of your previous battles have been if you are on the Black Tamer route. However, you should be well-equipped to deal with them if you prepared a little after the ritual.

CHAPTER 12 — Cleaning Up

Use the hidden Champion room exit to continue post-League content.

REVEAL — Chapter 12 details

After defeating the Pokémon League, there is a hidden door exit at the top-left of the Champion's room. Use it to exit.

CHAPTER 13 — Final Confrontation

Credits roll after final Cerulean Cave boss, but exploration continues.

REVEAL — Chapter 13 details

After you defeat the last boss in Cerulean Cave, the credits will roll, and you can still reload your last save to explore and find more secrets.

/// GENERAL TIPS

LISTEN CLOSELY

Re-read dialogue. Some ordinary characters have more to say on a second visit.

CHOICES LINGER

Opportunities can depend on decisions made many hours earlier.

EXPLORE FULLY

Optional rooms and routes often hold the most unsettling details.

BACK UP SAVES

Keep separate saves before any decision that feels final.

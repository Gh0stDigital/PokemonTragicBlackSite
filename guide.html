WALKTHROUGHA GUIDE FOR LOST TRAINERS

Spoiler policy: General guidance is shown openly. Anything that could spoil a twist, ending, or puzzle solution is tucked inside a “Reveal” dropdown — open it only when you're stuck.

Installing the patch SETUP

Supply your own legally obtained base ROM, then apply the release patch with a compatible patcher. Full steps and the verified ROM hash will appear on the Download page at launch.

Save often TIP

Some choices in this story cannot be undone. Keep multiple save slots so you can explore different paths without losing progress.
